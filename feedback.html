FROM THE AUTHOR

Tell me what you actually thought.

I'm writing Where the Light Falls, and Chapter One is where the story begins. If you've read it, I'd love to hear what stayed with you, what drew you in, and what didn't work.

Ideas for your feedback

You don't need to answer every question. Tell me whatever stood out.

  1. At what point did you want to keep reading? Did anything slow you down?
  2. What impression did you get of Elias and Mira?
  3. Could you picture Deepwater? Which details made it feel real, or left you confused?
  4. Did the mystery around Maren's document make sense and interest you?
  5. Would you want to read Chapter Two? Why or why not?
  6. Were there any awkward sentences, confusing speakers, or inconsistencies?
